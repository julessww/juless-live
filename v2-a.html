About me.

Before Zerion, I worked in fashion and venture capital, then founded a company. Fashion shaped my taste and storytelling instincts. VC taught me how companies grow. Founding taught me to execute with limited time, resources, and people.

I design experiences around how founders build trust and exchange ideas, not standard panels, mixers, or marketing playbooks. My work combines editorial judgment, operational rigor, and AI-native automation, applying human judgment where taste, nuance, and relationships matter.

In the last year, I produced 36 events that returned more than 2× their spend.
