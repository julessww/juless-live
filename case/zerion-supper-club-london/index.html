Around the table

Part of our monthly dinner program, London began with a reception and assigned seating. I planned a seat change halfway through so guests could meet more people without rushing each conversation. AI prompt cards gave guests something to explore and discuss with a partner.

What we learned

Clients asked for closer connections to the stablecoin ecosystem and discussed the API features they needed. Access to transaction data stood out as a priority.

Following up with clients

I gathered the feedback and sent personal follow-ups through Luma. With our commercial team, I decided which clients to reconnect with and invite to future dinners.
