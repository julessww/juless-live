Silence Please, New York

Off The Record Cafe

We hosted an all-day cafe centred on privacy at Silence Please in New York. I led the design assets and set design, from custom merchandise to the floral arrangements throughout the space.

Venue
Silence Please, NYC
Together with
Payy and Zerion

The design details

I was responsible for the design assets across custom Devoción coffee bags, notebooks, T-shirts, and menus.

Setting the space

I led the set design, bringing floral arrangements and branded details into the cafe. Calla lilies and moss ran through the table displays, alongside the custom coffee bags and printed pieces.

The guest list

I managed the guest list alongside the teams at Payy and Zerion, with 327 approved registrations for the day.
