Why we did it

Kraken was one of our key API partners. We wanted to meet more of the team in person and bring together existing clients, prospective partners, and founders. Access to investors was an important reason for founders to attend, so the audience included VCs.

What I led

I led the event with marketing support, working with our design lead on creative and operations on vendor coordination. The experience needed to serve both the founders in the room and the partners bringing them together.

What happened

Meeting much of Kraken’s team in person strengthened the relationship and helped open conversations about new opportunities. We also met people across New York’s startup ecosystem we had not connected with before and grew closer to local clients.

What came next

I gathered feedback from close partners and sent personalized follow-ups through Luma. With BD and enterprise, we reviewed priority contacts and discussed which partner relationships to continue through another event or collaboration.
