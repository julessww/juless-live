Bringing the partner teams together

Kraken was one of our key API partners. We wanted to meet more of the team in person alongside clients, prospective partners, and founders. We also brought VCs into the room because connecting with investors mattered to the founders attending.

Meeting much of Kraken’s team in person helped us get to know our partners and opened conversations about new opportunities. We also met founders we had not connected with before and spent time with our New York clients.

Continuing the conversation
