Why we did it

We wanted to connect with builders and hear how they were using products, with room for feedback outside formal building sessions.

What I led

I led the event with support from marketing, our design lead, and operations on vendor coordination. We alternated padel competition with company demos and dedicated build time, giving people different ways to meet and share their work.

What happened

The event welcomed 350+ attendees, with demos from 10 companies between matches. Competition and building were both part of the program.

How we followed through

Our event follow-up process included immediate feedback from close partners, personalized Luma messages, and a coordinated target list for BD and enterprise. We reviewed priority contacts together and considered where another event or collaboration would be useful.
