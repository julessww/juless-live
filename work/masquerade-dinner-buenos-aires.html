Buenos Aires

Masquerade Dinner

We co-hosted a rooftop masquerade dinner in Buenos Aires with teams we were exploring partnerships with. The evening moved from networking to dinner, then ended with a surprise party.

~50
Dinner guests

Bringing partners together

We split the dinner invitations across the co-hosting partners, bringing our guests together in one room. The event gave us time to connect with teams we hoped to build partnerships with.

From networking to the rooftop

Guests began with around 30 minutes of networking downstairs before moving to the rooftop for a two-hour dinner. We provided masquerade masks for everyone.

A surprise to close the night

After dinner, we brought everyone back downstairs for a surprise party. We coordinated two DJs playing back-to-back and custom cocktails for each sponsor. The full event ran for around six hours.
