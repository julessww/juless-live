Why we did it

London was part of a multi-city program hosting at least one dinner a month for key clients and the wider founder ecosystem. We wanted clients to meet us in person and give more builders a closer look at Zerion’s API, CLI, and wallet.

What I led

I led the experience with marketing support, our design lead on creative, and operations on vendor coordination. Guests met during an opening reception, sat in assigned places, and changed seats halfway through dinner.

A reverse-prompting activity gave everyone a card to explore with their AI agent of choice, then a response to discuss with a partner. Across cities, I learned how much guest selection and seating mattered to making a small group of founders connect.

What we learned

Conversations surfaced interest in stronger connections to the stablecoin ecosystem and the endpoints builders wanted from a provider like Zerion. The transaction endpoint emerged as particularly important. The dinner gave us a more specific understanding of what clients needed.

How we followed through

I gathered partner feedback, sent personalized Luma messages, and coordinated priority contacts with BD and enterprise. Together, we reviewed the next client conversations and which relationships could benefit from another event.
