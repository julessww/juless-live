Ensō Collective

Give the internet something to wear.

I founded Ensō Collective to connect fashion, technology, and digital identity. I led the brand partnerships, concepts, design, and production behind these digital wearables and physical merch collaborations.

I also led a six-person team building Ensō's iOS fashion app, which used AI to recognise brands and clothing and add them to a digital closet. The work brought product development and brand partnerships under one roof.

About me.

I lead marketing at Zerion, from our brand and voice to campaigns, partnerships, and events. I lead projects from the first brief through launch and run growth experiments to understand what gets results and why.

Before Zerion, I worked in fashion and venture capital, then founded a company. My work has included collaborations with Kraken, Revolut, SpaceX, and Privy, as well as partnerships with Coinbase.

I like turning a good idea into something people want to be part of, then figuring out what made it work.
